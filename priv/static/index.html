The target starts at the rate chosen here and can switch among the ones ticked: a Mac in Audio MIDI Setup, Windows in the sound device's properties. Applying reconnects the NanoKVM's USB devices on the target.

More USB devices

Each USB device takes some of the NanoKVM's endpoints: a network two IN and one OUT, the keyboard and mice one IN each, a storage device one of each however many drives it has, the microphone one IN, the speaker one OUT.

Virtual disk

An image goes in as what it holds: a disc image on the disc its format belongs on (CD, DVD or Blu-ray), any other image as a USB drive; the line under each image says what was found, and the choice can be forced. A folder goes in as a drive made from it: read-only, the computer sees the folder as it was; writable, what the computer changes comes back to the folder when the drive is ejected. Folders are exFAT drives, or FAT32 for BIOS updates, UEFI and older systems, which holds files of at most 4 GiB. Eject the drive on the computer before ejecting it here. The library is /data/images on the NanoKVM, which SFTP reaches too.
