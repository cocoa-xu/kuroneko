Auxiliary port

–

The USB-C port with the lightning icon carries two lines besides power. Off drives nothing, and the choice stays after a restart. Which contacts carry the lines, and at what voltage (most likely 3.3 V), is not confirmed yet: measure before connecting anything but the Finger Robot.

This system's kernel has no driver for the auxiliary port's lines.

Each part as the kernel sees it. Without a device the kernel made nothing of it: its driver is not built or its device tree node is off. A device without a driver failed to probe.

Restart the NanoKVM?

Video, keyboard and mouse stop for about 30 seconds. This page reconnects by itself.

USB network MAC addresses

Leave a field empty for the default, which comes from the NanoKVM's serial number.

USB network addresses

The NanoKVM's address and prefix: the computer gets the next address. Empty for the default.

The computer gets its new address when its end of the USB network comes back up. A page open over USB, as this one may be, then opens at the new address or at the NanoKVM's name.

Restarting…

The page comes back when the NanoKVM does.
